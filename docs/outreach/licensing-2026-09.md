# Licensing outreach drafts (Track 5b)

Drafted 2026-09-26 for Wilbur to edit and send. Nothing has been sent.

Why: the MTN Tactical and Uphill Athlete sessions in the archive (277 across the two, per
`PLAN-predictive-recommendations.md`) are personal-use reference material and cannot ship
in the app. Track 5 needs a licensed library before any cross-user ranking exists, so the
ask is a license to their sessions, or a clear no so the plan can move to a different
library.

Before sending, fill in the bracketed lines, confirm the contact route on each company's
site (neither address was looked up), and decide whether to offer terms up front or ask
for a call first. Both drafts ask for a call.

## What each company gets

- Their sessions placed inside a sport-season plan for athletes they do not reach today,
  credited by name, with a link to their paid plans.
- A choice of terms: a flat annual fee, a share of subscription revenue attributable to
  their content, or a pilot at no cost with a set review date.
- Later, and only if they want it: anonymous aggregates of how their sessions are
  completed, skipped and substituted. The app collects nothing today; this would ship
  with an opt-in and a privacy label change.
