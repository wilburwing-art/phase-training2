# Phase Training — Support

Phase Training is made by one person. Questions, bugs and requests are all welcome.

## Contact

- Email: [wilburwing@gmail.com](mailto:wilburwing@gmail.com)
- Or open an issue on the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/wilburwing-art/phase-training2/issues) (needs a free GitHub account)

For a bug, include your iPhone model, iOS version, the app version from Profile, and what you tapped just before it happened. Screenshots help.

## Common questions

**Where is my data?** On your phone. Workouts, plans and settings are stored on the device and included in your iPhone backup. Profile → Data has a manual Backup / Restore as well.

**Does the app write to Apple Health?** No. It reads workouts and, with a separate permission, body metrics. It never adds, changes or deletes anything in Health.

**How do I cancel Pro?** Pro is an App Store subscription. Cancel it in Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or from Profile → Subscription → Manage subscription. Access continues to the end of the period you paid for.

**Is the AI Coach on by default?** No. It is off until you turn it on and agree to what it sends. The [privacy policy](privacy.html) lists exactly what that is.
