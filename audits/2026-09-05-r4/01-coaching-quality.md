# R4 · 01 Coaching quality of the seven new content rows

## F1 (medium, fixed) — a 20 to 30 minute ruck inside a 45 minute strength circuit

Routine 302 opened with `Rucking (Zone-2 Walking with Pack) 1x20-30 min`
followed by six strength movements, declared 45 minutes. Work plus rest
summed to about 58 minutes, and the app's own estimator, which computes
`sets x 90 s`, showed about 28 minutes because it cannot see a duration-based
row. A loaded carry is a conditioning session and the sport-day side of the
plan already owns it. Replaced with `Lunge (Bodyweight) 3x10 per side`,
routine renamed "Mountaineering Descent and Leg Endurance", declared 40
minutes, description and notes rewritten so nothing still refers to a rucking
block. The estimator weakness is real but small (3 of 400 routine rows are
duration-based) and is filed as R3-12.

## F4 (info) — what the new content does to full-gym users

Adding a routine to a sport adds it to that sport's rotation for everyone,
not only the bodyweight tier. hiking-trekking, mountaineering and thru-hiking
now alternate Easy Strength with their new routine; snowboarding cycles three.
For a full-gym hiker that means a bodyweight session every other lift day,
which is a change in what they were getting. Defensible, since the new
routine is the sport-specific one and Easy Strength is generic, but it is a
change and not only an addition.

same movements, the variety observation R3 already made. Not a defect.
