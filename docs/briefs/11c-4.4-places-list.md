## Problem

The app takes one coarse fix (about 100 m) at each session start and keeps a
year of them (`PlanStore.sessionPlaces`, key `pt_session_places`). The only
reader is the DEBUG Signals sheet. A user cannot see what was stored, delete
it, or stop capture short of iOS Settings.
