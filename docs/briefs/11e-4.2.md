Research agents do not edit the repo. Each writes one file, `/tmp/combat-4.2/<group>.py`,
and returns a short report. The lead assembles `scripts/db/distill_combat.py`, runs `DB`,
adds the picker test and commits.

## Source standard (Phase 0 #4, accepted 2026-11-27)

- Author named with credentials or an institutional affiliation, and the venue named: a
  journal, a thesis, a book, a coach's own site, a governing body document, or a community
  page with its review count.
- Distill movements and doses only. Copy no prose. Notes are written in our words.
- Only use a page you fetched and read. Record the exact URL you read (DOI or PMC link
  preferred). If a full text is blocked, say so and do not distill from the abstract.
- One search pass per sport (about 10 to 15 fetches). A sport with no source meeting the
  standard is reported as "move to D" with the sources rejected and why.
- A source written for a sibling sport is fine when the paper says it applies (for example a
  karate paper that covers "striking martial arts"); say so in the note.

## What each routine needs

Pattern to copy: `scripts/db/distill_pilot_external.py` (read the `ROUTINES` list and the
module docstring). Each routine is a `dict(name=, phase=, goal=, minutes=, sports=,
credit=, url=, notes=, movements=[...])`.

- `phase`: one of `off_season`, `base`, `build`, `maintenance`. Pick from what the source
  says the block is for; when it names no season, say in the note that the phase is ours.
- `goal`: one of strength, power, endurance, direct_strength, antagonist, accessory.
  Never prehab, mobility, warm_up, pt_rehab, recovery.
- `minutes` >= 25 and at least 3 movements, or the app will not serve the routine.
- `sports`: a tuple of the ids above. A source that fits several sports in the group may tag
  several.
- `movements`: `(exercise_id, "Exact Catalog Name", sets, "reps", effort, "note")`. Look up
  ids with `sqlite3 db/coach.db "select id, name from exercises where name like '%row%'"`.
  The name must match the catalog exactly (the script checks). Effort is one of the
  constants STEADY, HARD, HEAVY, FAST. When the catalog has no row for the source's
  movement, use the nearest row and say "Source: <movement>" in the note. When no row is
  close, leave the movement out and name it in the routine note.
- Doses the source does not print are not invented. Say "the source gives no load" and give
  a plain working rule (for example "near a 5RM") in the note, as the pilot does.
- Target 2 or more qualifying routines per sport, ideally covering off-season or base,
  build and maintenance.

## Report back

1. The path of the file you wrote.
2. Per sport: routines written (name, phase), the citation, and how the source meets or
   falls short of the standard (one line).
3. Sports to move to D, with rejected sources and the reason for each.
