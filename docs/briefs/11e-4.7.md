## Source standard (Phase 0 #4, accepted 2026-11-27)

- Author named with credentials or an institutional affiliation, and the venue named: a
  journal, a thesis, a book, a coach's own site, a governing body document, or a community
  page with its review count. Professional athletes or national team coaches writing on a
  named outlet were accepted in 4.4 and 4.5.
- Distill movements and doses only. Copy no prose. Notes are written in our words.
- Only use a page you fetched and read. Record the exact URL you read (DOI or PMC link
  preferred). If a full text is blocked, say so and do not distill from the abstract.
- One search pass per sport (about 10 to 15 fetches). A sport with no source meeting the
  standard is reported as "move to D" with the sources rejected and why.
- A source written for a sibling sport is fine when the source says it applies (for example
  a paper on "riders" across disciplines); say so in the note.

## What each routine needs

Pattern to copy: `scripts/db/distill_movement_strength_dance.py` (read the `ROUTINES` list
and the module docstring). Each routine is a `dict(name=, phase=, goal=, minutes=, sports=,
credit=, url=, notes=, movements=[...])`, plus optional `environment=`.

- `phase`: one of `off_season`, `base`, `build`, `maintenance`. Pick from what the source
  says the block is for; when it names no season, say in the note that the phase is ours.
- `goal`: one of strength, power, endurance, direct_strength, antagonist, accessory.
  Never prehab, mobility, warm_up, pt_rehab, recovery.
- `minutes` >= 25 and at least 3 movements, or the app will not serve the routine. Do not
  stretch a 10 minute source to 25; drop it instead.
- No exercise id may appear twice in one routine (main CI rejects a repeated movement in a
  session, 11e 4.13). When a source lists two variants that map to one row, keep one row and
  put both doses in its note.
- `sports`: a tuple of the sport ids below.
- `movements`: `(exercise_id, "Exact Catalog Name", sets, "reps", effort, "note")`. Look up
  ids with `sqlite3 /Users/wilburpyn/repos/phase-training2/db/coach.db "select id, name from exercises where name like '%row%'"`.
  The name must match the catalog exactly (the lead's script checks). Effort is one of the
  constants STEADY, HARD, HEAVY, FAST. When the catalog has no row for the source's
  movement, use the nearest row and say "Source: <movement>" in the note. When no row is
  close, leave the movement out and name it in the routine note.
- Doses the source does not print are not invented. Say "the source gives no load" and give
  a plain working rule (for example "near a 5RM") in the note.
- Target 2 or more qualifying routines per sport, ideally covering off-season or base,
  build and maintenance.
- `environment`: "gym" (default), "home" or "anywhere". Off-horse, off-range S&C sessions
  only: riding lessons, shooting drills, running track sessions with no strength movement
  and pure cardio are not routines here.
- Routine ids are assigned by the script; do not pick ids. Name routines "<Sport>: <Session>".
- Your file defines only sport id tuples, credit/url strings and a `ROUTINES = [...]` list.
  Do not import anything and do not define the effort constants.

  S&C paper written for "riders" in general may tag all five riding slugs above; one written
  for one discipline (for example show jumpers) tags that slug only unless it says it covers

  masters sprinters, a governing body document such as World Masters Athletics, or a

- Dog agility: a source on the handler's conditioning (the human), not the dog's.
- Canyoneering: canyoneering-named sources first; a mountaineering or climbing program only
  if the source says it applies to canyoneering.
